* Lines

3. Every new transmission follows `PHRASES.md`: the voice is never threatening, the joke or the dread is never explained, and the caller's history stays consistent.
4. Every change to the phrase list lands everywhere it lives: `HiddenMachine.dc.html` and `index.html` together, `PHRASE-POOL.md` regenerated, and the receiver assets regenerated from `dc.html`.
5. The station stays one self-contained static page: no build step, no analytics or trackers, and no calls to outside servers.

* Backlog
One heading per item. Under it: an optional property drawer (=:PROPERTIES:= to =:END:=, directly under the heading), then =Why:=, one line saying what the item serves in the project (it becomes the middle link of a builder prompt's INTENT), then one line of scores and evidence, then optional =Waits on:= prose. =:BLOCKED_BY:= is the exact heading text of another Backlog item, several in one value separated by a pipe with a space each side (=Item one | Item two=); =:PROMPT:= is the filename, without =.md=, of the builder prompt queued for this item; =:AFTER:= is the exact heading text of an item planned to come before this one, one =:AFTER:= line per predecessor, an order and never a date; =:REJUDGE:= is the path of what the idea room must judge afresh for this item. An item is done when its heading starts with =Done=.
